All six opened. Scribd blocked automated access, so P2 was read page by page in the browser. P3 and P6 use the old Limon/Truth Khmer font encoding and were converted to Unicode. P4's text layer was broken, so it was OCR'd.

**Where the provided sources disagree:** S-21 (17,000 / 7 survivors in P1; 14,000 / 23 in P3; 14,000 / 12 in P6) · death toll (P1 up to 2.5 m; P3 & P6 "about/nearly 2 million") · constitution details (P2, P5) · Sihanouk's resignation date · the Four-Year Plan years · whether ethnic Chinese were singled out (P3 no; P1 & P6 list them as victims) · framing of 17 April and 7 January ("liberation" in P2, P4, P5; neutral in P1, P6).
