# Music and sound plan · Democratic Kampuchea 1975–1979: We Remember

Original score, synthesised for this film (engine/music70.py moods; nothing copied). Instruments: **solo tro** (Khmer bowed fiddle), **soft khim** (hammered dulcimer), **quiet strings**, a low drone, sparse piano, and a **distant monastery bell**. The music ducks about 80 % under every voice. Master at −16 LUFS.
