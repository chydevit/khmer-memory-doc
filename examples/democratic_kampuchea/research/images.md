# Democratic Kampuchea (17 Apr 1975 – 7 Jan 1979): archive image research

Researched **2026-10-25**. For every Commons file the licence was read on the file page (wikitext licence template and the API `extmetadata`). Thumbnails, and full-size crops where needed, were opened to check that each caption matches the picture. Downloaded files are in `archive/img/` (long side ≤ 4000 px, EXIF rotation applied), and their full records are in `archive/manifest.json`.

**Sensitivity rules applied:** no corpses, skulls, bones, torture or execution images were collected. No S-21 prisoner portraits were downloaded: they are listed only with the permission route. Choeung Ek is represented by stupa exteriors only. Tuol Sleng is represented by building exteriors, barbed wire, empty classrooms and corridors, and the rules board. The iron-bed rooms were excluded because 1979 photos of the dead hang on their walls.

Status key: **USABLE (downloaded)** = in `img/`. **USABLE (alt.)** = free, but not downloaded. **PERMISSION REQUIRED** = may be listed but must not be used until written permission arrives. **EXCLUDED** = graphic, or the licence is false or doubtful.

## Gaps (draw these and label them "Reconstruction")

1. **The KR fighters entering Phnom Penh on 17 April 1975** (black clothes, crowds, white flags). Only AP and Neveu photos exist. *Free substitute:* the US Air Force aerial photos of 17 April (bridge, burning depot).
2. **The forced evacuation of the cities, 17–20 April 1975**: crowds on Monivong Boulevard and Route 5, hospital patients pushed in beds. No free photo. *Base:* DMA 1971 city map, CIA 1972 population map.
3. **Evacuation routes map:** no free period map exists, so draw it on the DMA/PCL base maps using published evacuation routes (DC-Cam, Yale CGP, Ponchaud/Kiernan).
4. **"Year Zero" scenes:** the banking system abolished (banknotes printed but never issued), the National Bank blown up, markets shut, schools closed, pagodas emptied and monks disrobed. Free substitutes: the CIA NPIC 1977 report (cathedral razed), the 1930 monks photo (before), the DK flag. No free image of DK banknotes.
5. **Angkar meetings, self-criticism sessions and children's units.**
6. **Cooperative life:** dam and canal building by thousands, communal kitchens, rice rations, rest-day propaganda meetings. Only propaganda films and photos exist, all needing permission. *Free substitutes:* the Pol Pot-era canal (2013), the KR clothing display, the S-21 soup bowl, Wat Ek (1999).

8. **Son Sen, Ta Mok, So Phim, Ieng Thirith (in the DK period):** no free portraits. (Ta Mok's house stands in.)

## Flags: misleading captions, wrong dates, false or doubtful licences

1. **Romanian 1978 photos were taken in Phnom Penh, not Romania.** "Nicolae Ceaușescu with Pol Pot.jpg" (and its crops "PolPot.jpg", "Pol Pot (cropped).jpg", "Khieu Samphan 1978.jpg") come from Ceaușescu's *official visit to Democratic Kampuchea*, 28–30 May 1978 (the Romanian caption reads "a făcut o vizită oficială în Kampuchia"). Our earlier `khmer_republic_doc/research/images.md` called Khieu Samphan's photo "in Romania, 1978". That is wrong.
2. **"Ieng Sary in 1978.jpg" is misdated in its file name:** the photo is from 15 Oct 1976 (Ieng Sary received by Ceaușescu in Bucharest).
3. **"Photos of young Khmer Rouge fighters (on display at the Tuol Sleng Museum).jpg"** actually shows the wall of S-21 *prisoner portraits*. Wrong caption, and the PD-user licence cannot cover the museum's photos. Not downloaded.
4. **"MONATIO Soldiers.jpg"** (17 Apr 1975) credits Christoph Froehder (AP) but is tagged CC BY 4.0. The licence is false: it is an AP copyright photo.
5. **"H 4 ill 639759 cambodia-phnom penh-1979-61.jpg"** ("Vietnamese soldiers entering Phnom Penh, Jan 1979") is uploaded as "own work" CC BY-SA. That claim is implausible for a 1979 press photo, and the event is unverified.
6. **"Pol Pot in 1978.png"** (PD-RO-photo) is nominated for deletion on Commons (24 Oct 2026). There is no evidence the photographer was Romanian. Do not use.
7. **AI-enhanced DK images** ("Танкісти / Солдатки-зенітниці / Солдати Революційної армії Кампучії") are marked "enhanced with AI", carry CC0 claims and have deletion requests open. Not authentic at pixel level.
8. **"Cambodia 2011 monuments 21.jpg"** is captioned "memorial stupa" but is a close-up of the skulls. Excluded.
9. **"Tuol Sleng Genocide Museum Vientiane, Cambodia (11958426624).jpg"** puts Vientiane in Cambodia (Vientiane is the capital of Laos). It is a Phnom Penh photo.
10. **"Cathédrale de Phnom Penh.jpg"** is a pre-1975 aerial photo uploaded as "own work" (2018). Doubtful licence.
11. **"Decapitated statues at Angkor ... destroyed by the Khmer Rouge"** blames the KR without evidence (most Angkor beheadings were done by looters).
12. **"Wat Ek killing yard at Arn Chorn-Pond's child labor camp ... 1999-04.jpg"**: the title says "killing yard", but the photo shows a temple hall used as a dormitory. Do not reuse the title as a caption.
13. **"Cambodia. Phnom Penh. ... Bou Meng one of two adult survivors ..."** About seven adult S-21 survivors are known. In 2018 Bou Meng and Chum Mey were the last two *living*.
14. **"View of Phnom Penh from CH-53.jpg" and the Eagle Pull photos** are from 12 April 1975, five days before the fall. Never caption them "17 April".
15. **"Fall of Phnom Penh.jpg"** is dated 1984 on Commons, but the Sak Sutsakhan monograph was published in 1980.
16. **"29 August 2011 Courtroom (1–3).jpg"** (ECCC): the EXIF date is 2010-08-28 (camera clock); the caption date of 29 Aug 2011 is correct.
17. **1970 refugee photos** ("Vietnamese refugees from Cambodia ... RVNS Da Nang", Jul 1970) show the Lon Nol-era expulsions. They would be misleading for the 1975 DK expulsion of Vietnamese.
18. **"Khmers rouges map.png"** maps KR activity in 1989–90, not 1975–79.
19. **"Choeung Ek commemorative stupa filled with skulls.jpg" / "Skulls from the killing fields.jpg"** are tagged PD with no author or source. Doubtful (and graphic: excluded).
20. **USAF 17 April 1975 aerial photos:** the licence is credible (USAF), but the Commons source is a private Google Drive folder rather than a NARA catalogue record. Try to match them in NARA before broadcast.
21. **"Rosalynn Carter ... Sa Kas, Thailand"**: "Sa Kas" is a misspelling of Sa Kaeo.

## Notes on licences used

- **US government works** (USAF, USMC, NARA, CIA, White House/Carter Library, VOA staff, LoC Federal Research Division, DMA): public domain. Credit is given as good practice.
- **ECCC Commons files** (CC BY 2.0 / CC BY-SA 2.0): credit "ECCC / photographer". Share-alike applies to the BY-SA files if they are used as images in a derivative work.
- **Fototeca online a comunismului românesc** (Commons tag FOCR): free use, but the citation is *mandatory*: photo number, database name, date of access, ANIC reference.
- **Bernard Gotfryd (LoC)**: no known copyright restrictions (PD-Gotfryd). Privacy and publicity rights may apply.
- **Nationaal Archief / Anefo**: CC0.
- **CC BY-SA modern photos** (Tuol Sleng, Choeung Ek, Cham village, maps): give the attribution line from the manifest and license the film's credits accordingly.
