Compiled 2026-10-25 (all sources accessed 2026-10-25). Scope: Ch.6 targeted groups · Ch.7 S-21/Tuol Sleng · Ch.8 purges · Ch.9 war and collapse · Ch.10 aftermath · death toll.

**Status key**
- ✓✓ means at least two independent sources agree.
- ✓ (1) means one good source only.
- ⚖ DISPUTED means sources disagree; each version is given.
- ✗ UNVERIFIED means do not use.

Two pages from the same publisher count as one source. ECCC judgments, ECCC press releases and ECCC Court Reports all count as one publisher.

**Verdict on the user's "1.38 to 2.5 million": CORRECT IT.**
- The low end comes from mass-grave mapping. That method counts remains in graves, mostly execution victims. It is estimated rather than exhumed, and is not a total death toll. The "1,386,734" figure itself is unverified.
- The high end, Heuveline's 2.52 million, covers **the whole decade 1970–79**. Heuveline's own DK-only figure is about 2.2 million.

**Recommended narration wording:**
- "**Between about 1.5 and 2 million people — roughly one in five Cambodians — died** under the Khmer Rouge, from execution, starvation, overwork and disease." This matches the ECCC 002/02 para 297, Kiernan's 21–24% and Heuveline's 2015 median of 21%.
- Alternatively: "**Close to two million**" (DC-Cam).
- If a range is needed: "estimates range from about 1.5 to 2.2 million" (Kiernan / Tabeau-Kheam / Heuveline 2001 DK-only).

## Disputed points (each side)

1. **S-21 prisoner numbers.**
   - ECCC Case 001: ≥12,273 detained and ≥12,272 died. ECCC 002/02: ≥11,742 executed. Both are minimums from surviving lists.
   - Tuol Sleng Museum: 15,000–20,000. DC-Cam/Dy: about 14,000.
   - **Use "at least 12,000, probably many more".**
2. **S-21 survivors.**
   - 7 (media and AP).
   - About 12, including 4 children (Museum; Dy).
   - 202, including 179 earlier releases (DC-Cam 2011).
   - Duch said the release lists were faked.
   - **Use "only a handful survived".**
3. **So Phim's death.** June (2 or 3 Jun) 1978 per ECCC/S-21 notebooks, versus May 1978 per Dy 2007.
4. **Koy Thuon's arrest.** Sent to S-21 in Jan 1977 (ECCC) versus "arrested and executed in 1976" (Dy).
5. **Blue scarves.** That deportees wore them is attested. Kiernan says they were deliberately issued as a marker. Heder says they were not planned as a marker.
6. **Front founding place (2 Dec 1978).** Snuol, Kratie (Dy/DC-Cam, Cambodian government) versus Ho Chi Minh City (ECCC E465 para 293).
7. **Start of the Vietnamese offensive.** 25 Dec 1978 (Dy, Keesing's) versus 22 Dec 1978 (LoC).
8. **1975 island clashes.** Who struck first: Vietnamese and DK propaganda conflict, and the ECCC did not decide. **1977 large incursions**: Vietnam's claims were not proven beyond reasonable doubt (ECCC); Dy and LoC report civilian deaths.
9. **Cham population and losses.** 191,000 / 250,000 / 700,000 before 1975, and about 11,000 to 500,000 deaths (see Ch.6).
10. **Hou Youn's fate.** 1975 versus 1976.
11. **Choeung Ek details.** Exhumed 8,895 vs 8,985; graves excavated 86 vs 89 of 129 or 130; stupa 1988 vs 1989; distance 13 vs 15 km.
12. **CIA 1980 estimate.** 1.2–1.8 m population drop (ECCC quote of the report) versus "1.4 million" (Dy).
13. **Sliwinski's total.** 1.71 m (Green Left) / 1.843–1.871 m (Kiernan) / 1.9 m (Heuveline).
14. **Tuol Po Chrey / "kill all Lon Nol soldiers" policy.** The SCC (2016) found such a policy unproven for April 1975. The Trial Chamber 002/02 nevertheless found persecution of former Republic officials. **Do not say "all soldiers were to be killed".**
15. **Remembrance-day letter year.** VOA says a 2011 government letter; the government document refers to correspondence of 2013.

## DO-NOT-SAY list

- ✗ "The Khmer Rouge killed 3 million / 3.3 million people." This is the 1983 PRK figure, judged overstated.
- ✗ "1.38 to 2.5 million died" (see the correction above). ✗ "2.5 million died under Pol Pot": 2.52 m is Heuveline's figure for 1970–79.
- ✗ "1.7 million people were executed." Most estimates put violent deaths at about 0.8–1.3 m; the rest died of starvation, disease and overwork. Don't call all deaths "killings".
- ✗ "The ECCC found the Khmer Rouge committed genocide against the Khmer people / Cambodians." The genocide findings concern the **Vietnamese** (final) and the **Cham** (trial judgment only; Nuon Chea; not final).
- ✗ "Khieu Samphan was convicted of genocide against the Cham." He was acquitted of that; his conviction concerns the Vietnamese.
- ✗ "The court confirmed Cham genocide on appeal." There was no appeal judgment because Nuon Chea died.
- ✗ "Only 7 people survived S-21" stated as fact. Use "only a handful survived".
- ✗ "20,000 people were killed at Tuol Sleng" stated as a court finding. The court minimum is 12,272.
- ✗ "Tuol Sleng was a high school called Tuol Sleng." It was Ponhea Yat / Tuol Svay Prey High School, plus Tuol Sleng Primary School.
- ✗ "S-21 prisoners were mostly Lon Nol officials." Most were Khmer Rouge cadres and soldiers (ECCC, Dy).
- ✗ "The Khmer Rouge planned to kill all former soldiers" stated as fact (see disputed point 14).
- ✗ "The SCC appeal judgment was on 23 September 2022." It was pronounced **22 Sep 2022**; the written judgment is dated 23 Dec 2022.
- ✗ "So Phim was executed." He shot himself to avoid arrest (June 1978).
- ✗ "Vietnam invaded unprovoked" or "Vietnam liberated Cambodia" as the narrator's own judgment. Say neutrally: "Vietnamese forces, with the Front, entered Cambodia; Phnom Penh fell on 7 January 1979."
- ✗ Graphic descriptions of interrogation or killing methods (the film is symbolic).
- ✗ "20 May is a public holiday." It has not been since 2020; say "is observed as the National Day of Remembrance".
- ✗ "Ieng Thirith was acquitted." She was found unfit to stand trial.
